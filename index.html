About the Workshop

Recent advances in digital pathology and molecular profiling have created a new type of large-scale biomedical data that combines extremely large tissue images, molecular measurements such as gene or protein signals, and associated clinical information. Analyzing these data is challenging because they come from different sources, have different formats, and are often incomplete or noisy.

This workshop brings together researchers from machine learning, data mining, computational pathology, bioinformatics, and industry to discuss methods for integrating image, molecular, and clinical data. Our goal is to develop scalable and interpretable approaches that better support biomarker discovery, patient stratification, and translational research.

Call for Papers

We invite submissions of original, unpublished research on big data methods for computational pathology and multi-omics. We welcome methodological contributions, systems and scalability work, benchmarks and datasets, and application papers with clear translational relevance. Accepted papers will be presented at the workshop and published in the {{ site.event.conference }} workshop proceedings.

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must be submitted as a PDF in the IEEE 2-column format, following the IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Manuscript Formatting Guidelines (templates).
  • Full papers may be up to 10 pages and short papers up to 5 pages, including references, figures, and tables.
  • Reviews are single-blind: please include author names and affiliations in the submission.
  • Submissions must be original work that is not published or under review elsewhere.
  • Accepted papers will be published in the {{ site.event.conference }} workshop proceedings by the IEEE Computer Society Press and made available in IEEE Xplore.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the conference and present the paper in person at the workshop in {{ site.event.city }}.
